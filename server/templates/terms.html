Terms & privacy

glow.lighting makes your phone one light in the show. Tapping Tap to start means you agree to this page.

Flashing lights. Screens and flashes around you can flash and change colour quickly. If you have photosensitive epilepsy or are sensitive to flashing light, please don't take part, and look away.

What the page does

It shows colours full screen, sent live from the lighting desk, and keeps your screen awake. If your phone can and you allow it, it also switches your camera flash with the show. Nothing is installed: close the page or switch apps and it all stops.

The camera

A web page can only reach the flash through the camera, so your phone asks first. The camera is used only to switch the flash: no photo or video is taken, viewed, stored or sent, though your phone shows the camera as in use. Don't allow still gives you the screen; you can change it in your browser's site settings.

What is logged

A random id kept on your phone, your IP address, browser and device type, screen size, language, time zone, connection time and network delay. No name, contacts, location or photos; no ads or trackers. Used only to run and improve the show, and {% if retention_days %}deleted after {{ retention_days }} days{% else %}kept until the crew clears it{% endif %}.

Be considerate

Don't point your flash at people's eyes, the performers or the stage. Follow venue staff, who may ask you to stop at any time, and mind your surroundings and belongings.
