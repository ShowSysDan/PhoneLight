Terms & privacy

glow.lighting turns your phone into one light in the show. This page explains what it does and what you agree to when you tap Tap to start.

Flashing lights. The show can flash and change colour quickly, on your screen and on the phones around you. If you have photosensitive epilepsy or are sensitive to flashing light, please don't take part, and look away from the screens.

What the page does

  • Shows colours full screen, sent live from the show's lighting desk, and keeps your screen awake while it is open.
  • If your phone supports it and you allow it, switches your camera flash on and off with the show.
  • Nothing is installed. Close the page, or switch to another app, and it all stops.

The camera permission

Web pages can only reach the flash through the camera, so your phone asks to let the page use the camera. Tap Allow to have your flash join the show. The camera is used only to switch the flash: no photo or video is ever taken, looked at, stored or sent anywhere. Your phone shows that the camera is in use while the page is open. Tap Don't allow and the show still works on your screen. You can change your mind in your browser's site settings.

What is logged

To run the show, the server logs basic technical details: a random id this page keeps on your phone (so we know when a phone comes back), your IP address, browser and device type, screen size, language, time zone, how long you are connected and the network delay. We do not ask for your name, contacts, location, photos or anything personal, and the page has no ads or trackers. These details are used only to run and improve the show, and are {% if retention_days %}deleted after {{ retention_days }} days{% else %}kept until the show's crew clears them{% endif %}.

Be considerate

  • Don't point your flash into people's eyes, at the performers, or at the stage.
  • Follow the venue's rules and staff; they may ask you to stop at any time.
  • Keep an eye on your surroundings and your belongings.
